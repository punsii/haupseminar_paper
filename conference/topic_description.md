# Feasibility of Generating Gaussian Splats in Real Time

This project tests whether dynamic Gaussian splats can be learned online in real
time, aiming at the scene's general shape rather than its detail.
It aims to give an overview of methods in the literature for speeding up splat training.
As a practical application, one of these methods is evaluated by
training on the same scenes with and without it.
The variants are compared mainly on latency and frame rate,
with reconstruction quality as a secondary criterion.
