# GSC-ECAISEVCML27
## Conference on current topics in applied information technology

GSC-ECAISEVCML27 is a premier, top-tier conference focusing on optimizing procedures in embedded machine learning systems for signal processing and security.
A holistic understanding of IT-systems is essential for securing prosperity for humanity.

GSC-ECAISEVCML27 welcomes papers on secondary research that explore the feasibility of proof of concept implementations.
Our goal is collecting and comparing results over a wide selection of topics in order to ensure the validity and integrity of research in information technology.

## Symposium Topics
* Machine Learning
  - Large Language Models
  - Object Detection
  - Neural Rendering
* Embedded Systems
  - Signal Processing
  - Efficiency
* Security
